How the sync actually works

Every tap is a small timestamped fact — “Max carried,” “defense series, these eight played” — queued on this phone the instant you tap it, so nothing ever waits on a network to feel responsive. Syncing sends your queued facts to the shared file and pulls in anyone else's; the app then recomputes every total from the full combined set. Two coaches tapping at the same time never overwrite each other — their facts just both end up in the file.

If a sync fails — no signal, expired token — your taps stay queued right here and nothing is lost; try “Sync now” again once you're back in range. The one edge case worth knowing: if someone else ends the game on their phone before you've synced, your still-queued taps for that game can't be merged in after the fact — the app will tell you if that happens so you can double-check the season table.
